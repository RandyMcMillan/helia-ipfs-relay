This page runs a complete Helia IPFS node (libp2p + bitswap) in your browser. It serves circuit relay v2 reservations for peers that reach it, and makes itself dialable by reserving /p2p-circuit slots on public relays. Your peer identity persists in localStorage; the blockstore is in-memory and resets on reload.
